Frameworks of Speech

Master the architecture of impromptu excellence.

The PREP Method

The gold standard for clear, persuasive opinions. Use this when you need to state a position and back it up instantly.

Point

State your main idea or stance clearly and directly. Don't bury the lead.

Reason

Explain the 'why' behind your point. Provide the logic that supports your stance.

Example

Provide a concrete story, data point, or real-world case that illustrates your reason.

Point

Restate your original point with fresh conviction to drive the message home.

The STAR Method

The essential framework for storytelling and interviews. It transforms a rambling anecdote into a structured narrative of success.

Situation

Set the scene. Briefly describe the context and the challenge you faced.

Task

Explain your specific responsibility in that situation. What was the goal?

Action

Describe the specific steps you took. This should be the 'meat' of your response.

Result

Share the outcome. Be specific about the impact and what was learned.

The Rule of Three

The human brain loves patterns. Grouping your supporting points into three makes your speech inherently more memorable and authoritative.

Opening

Introduce your topic and explicitly state that you have three key points to share.

The Points

Walk through your three points. Point 1, Point 2, Point 3. Keep them distinct.

Closing

Briefly summarize the three points and tie them back to your main message.

Rhythm

The Rule of Three creates a natural cadence that feels complete and satisfying to the listener.

The SEE-I Method

A powerful tool for clarifying complex ideas. Use this to ensure your audience truly understands the essence of what you're saying.

State

Make your claim or define your concept in one clear, concise sentence.

Elaborate

Unpack the idea. Explain it in more detail using your own words.

Exemplify

Provide a concrete, real-world example that shows the idea in action.

Illustrate

Use an analogy, metaphor, or mental image that makes the concept 'stick'.

The SCORE Framework

The ultimate strategic thinking tool. Use this to analyze complex problems, evaluate options, and provide a clear recommendation for action.

Situation

Briefly set the context. What is the current state of affairs?

Challenges

Identify the core problem or obstacles. What is standing in the way of progress?

Options

List potential solutions or paths forward. What are the choices available?

Recommendation

Select the best path forward. Which option do you advocate for and why?

Execution

Outline the next steps for success. How do we turn this recommendation into reality?

What-So What-Now What

A simple, high-impact framework for providing updates, reflections, or evaluating events.

What

Describe the event, the topic, or the situation as it stands right now.

So What

Explain the significance. Why does this matter? What is the impact?

Now What

Define the next steps. What should happen next? What is the call to action?

Clarity

This framework forces you to move beyond description and into meaningful action.
